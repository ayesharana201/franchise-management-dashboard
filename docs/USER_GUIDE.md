# User Guide

Sign in at `/login`. The left menu shows every area; what you can see and do inside each depends on your role.

## Franchisor (head office)

- **Dashboard** — network KPIs, monthly sales chart, location leaderboard, and an attention panel (overdue compliance, orders awaiting approval, documents expiring within 60 days). Use the date range at the top to change the window.
- **Locations** — add, edit, or remove locations and set each location's royalty %, marketing fee %, and monthly technology fee.
- **Users & Roles** — create accounts. Franchisees are tied to one location; regional managers to one region; franchisors see everything. You cannot demote or delete your own account.
- **Royalties & Fees** — review all statements, filter by status, and click **Mark paid** when payment is received.
- **Compliance** — create checklist items, then assign an item to one location or all locations with a due date.
- **Inventory** — maintain the supply catalogue (price, stock, reorder level). Low-stock items are highlighted.
- **Messages** — send a message to one location, or leave "To" on *All locations* to post an announcement.
- **Documents** — upload network-wide policies or location-specific contracts, with optional expiry dates.
- **Financial Reports** — pick a location and date range; export CSV.

## Regional manager

Same screens as the franchisor, limited to locations in your region. You can assign compliance checks, approve and update supply orders, mark royalties paid, and message your locations, but you cannot create locations, edit the catalogue, or manage users.

## Franchisee / location manager

- **Dashboard** — your location's performance.
- **Royalties & Fees** — use the *Fee calculator* to estimate fees for any sales figure. At month end use **Submit monthly sales** (gross sales and, optionally, costs). Your statement is generated automatically and is due on the 15th of the following month. Re-submitting before it's paid updates it.
- **Compliance** — set each assigned check to *Passed* or *Failed* and add notes.
- **Supply Orders** — **New order**, enter quantities (totals update live), and submit. You can't order more than is in stock. Head office updates the status through approved → shipped → delivered.
- **Messages** — contact head office and read announcements and replies.
- **Documents** — view and download your contracts and network policies; upload your own (PDF, Word, or image up to 10 MB).
- **Financial Reports** — your monthly profit and loss.

## Notifications

Each morning the system flags unpaid statements past their due date as **overdue** and emails the location's franchisee once.
